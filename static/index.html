For stallholders and markets

Selling: put your stall online

1. Walk the stalls

Upload a video or photo of a stall, or film live. NVIDIA Nemotron VL reads the items and price cards, and the stock list below fills in.

Back to the demo market

2. Check the prices

Looks up each item on UK supermarket websites (web search) and flags a price that looks wrong. It never changes a stall's price.

3. End-of-day deals

NVIDIA's promotion agent suggests a discount for stock that's left over. Code applies it and never goes below the stall's margin floor. Stock counts here are made up.

4. Let AI agents buy

Turns the stock list into product records in the ACP and UCP formats AI shopping agents use. Anything flagged is held back.
