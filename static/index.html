How it works

    
1. Phone walkFilm the stalls, or upload a video or photo.

    
2. NVIDIA Nemotron VL on Nebius Token FactoryReads every frame: items, price cards, stall signs.

    
3. Stock listCode merges what it saw and owns every price. Anything unclear is flagged, never guessed.

    
4. Voice agent and web estimatesAsk for a dish: it checks the stockroom, prices the stalls, and estimates the rest from UK supermarkets.
